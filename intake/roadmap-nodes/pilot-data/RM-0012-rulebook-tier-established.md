`IRuleBook` relocated to `LunaFlow.Rulebook` in Phase 2.6.5 — that project *is* the Rulebook tier
rather than an example holder (`Documents/design/glossary.md`'s **Rulebook** entry). OneD20 is the one
Rulebook that exists: a specific game's rules (combat sequencing, win conditions) built in C# on top
of ROM tools, by a Rulebook Author rather than a ROM Developer — see the glossary's **Rulebook
Author** entry for how that role differs from a **ROM Developer**.

**Backfill caveat, added 2026-08-08 — this pilot's own version of RM-0001's "practical root" note.**
Created after the fact, alongside RM-0013, to give "a Rulebook exists and depends on a ROM" a place
in the graph, at Arryn's direction. Unlike RM-0002 through RM-0008, it isn't tied to a specific
handoff ticket or QA gate — its evidence is the glossary entry cited above, not a verdict document.
The real event it represents overlaps Phase 2.6.5 (the same phase RM-0001 covers), modeled as a
separate node here anyway since it's a conceptually distinct milestone (Rulebook tier established,
not ROM identity/interop) — consistent with this pilot already treating its own earlier history as
incomplete.
